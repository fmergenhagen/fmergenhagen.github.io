I am a PhD researcher in economics at the [German Institute for Economic Research (DIW Berlin)](https://www.diw.de/sixcms/detail.php?id=diw_01.c.976127.en), Department of Energy, Transportation, Environment, and at the [Workgroup for Infrastructure Policy (WIP)](https://www.tu.berlin/wip) at Technische Universität Berlin.

My research mainly covers network flow problems, mostly in resource and electricity markets. I additionally have field-based expertise in energy access and the associated welfare and development questions.

For more information, see my [CV](/files/CV_Mergenhagen.pdf).

## Research interests

- Energy & Environmental Economics
- Energy Access
- Network Flow Problems

**Methods:** optimization and energy system modelling (Python, Julia, PyPSA), spatial analysis (QGIS).
