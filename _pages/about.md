I am a PhD researcher in economics at the [German Institute for Economic Research (DIW Berlin)](https://www.diw.de/sixcms/detail.php?id=diw_01.c.976127.en), Department of Energy, Transportation, Environment, and at the [Workgroup for Infrastructure Policy (WIP)](https://www.tu.berlin/wip) at Technische Universität Berlin. My dissertation, *Essays in Energy Economics*, is supervised by Prof. Dr. Christian von Hirschhausen; my qualification phase is part of the Berlin School of Economics.

My research mainly covers network flow problems, mostly in resource and electricity markets. I additionally have field-based expertise in energy access and the associated welfare and development questions. For my master's thesis, written in collaboration with GIZ in Madagascar, I developed a least-cost electrification model for around 200,000 residents of Antongil Bay, including one month of fieldwork.

I hold a master's degree in Environmental Policy (economics track) from Duke University and a bachelor's degree in Economics from the University of Hamburg. In between, I took graduate coursework in environmental and political economics at Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne.

## Research interests

- Energy economics and electricity markets
- Network flow problems in resource markets
- Energy access and development
- Environmental economics

**Methods:** optimization and energy system modelling (Python, Julia, PyPSA), spatial analysis (QGIS).
